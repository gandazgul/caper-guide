# Key moments

Harness-specific guidance informed by
[jwynia's key-moments skill](https://github.com/jwynia/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/creative/fiction/structure/key-moments/SKILL.md).
This is an original adaptation for a prewritten, player-directed adventure, not the upstream story-outline
workflow.

## Find what deserves emphasis

Consider the experience a situation invites: curiosity, relief, unease, discovery, or another feeling suited
to the agreed tone. Choose a few especially promising moments rather than making every scene a climax. Look
for a concrete image, interaction, or change in understanding that gives each moment its identity. Contrast
intense passages with room to explore and respond. Check that preparation and consequences connect plausibly.

## Author: prepare an opportunity, not a requirement

Start with the source and approved assets, not a genre checklist. Identify which existing scene can support
the moment, its entry conditions, what players may perceive, and which decisions or state changes could
follow. Keep these notes in the relevant scene's supported fields through author_upsert_scene; do not create a
separate beat-tracking file or invent new schema fields.

Treat emotional intent as a presentation suggestion, not a promise of player feelings. A source-backed
discovery may invite wonder without requiring a character to be awestruck. If a moment depends on a missing
fact, reveal, or route, flag that gap for the author instead of manufacturing evidence. Proposals remain
adaptations, with source context clearly distinguished from new material.

## Guide: let the actual game determine the moment

Use authored emphasis only when the current situation and state support it. Allow a moment to change, occur in
a permitted order, or never occur because of player decisions. Do not relocate clues, reverse a success, add a
betrayal, or force resource loss to complete an emotional sequence. Do not narrate the players' fear, trust,
realization, or character growth for them.

At a significant event, make the observable change clear and leave space for a response. An unexpected but
legitimate resolution deserves its own ending; do not prolong the adventure solely to reach prepared scenes.
Pacing never bypasses reveal permissions, mechanical updates, or agreed content boundaries.
